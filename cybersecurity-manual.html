One AI agent. Five moves. Zero humans.

This is the chain reported in the Anthropic disclosure everyone's sharing right now — an AI running an espionage-style operation mostly on its own. Every wall in this manual exists to break one of these moves.

Recon

Probe

Impersonate

Cloned voices and video ask your people for money, codes, and access.

MOVE 04

Exploit

Copies data, drains accounts, locks files — then does it again tomorrow. It never gets tired.
