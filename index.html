The $10,000-a-Month Blueprint.

A visible, repeatable path from one useful service to a $10,000 gross monthly target—built on delivery and documented value, never promises of secret money or guaranteed income.

2 × 4projects per week × four weeks

One clear offer. Eight completed projects.

Start with a bounded service you can deliver well: a records-organization setup, a private local-AI office workflow, a document-integrity pack or an operations binder. At an average package price of $1,250, eight completed projects gross $10,000.

Step 1 · Package

Sell a defined result

Name the deliverables, timeline, exclusions and fixed package price. Do not market legal, tax or investment advice.

Step 2 · Prospect

Create a weekly pipeline

Focus outreach on organizations already burdened by scattered records, repetitive paperwork or weak version control.

Step 3 · Deliver

Use one documented process

Intake, inventory, organize, verify, hand off and obtain written acceptance. Keep private source files off-chain.

Step 4 · Repeat

Complete two per week

Collect feedback, referrals and proof of process—then improve the same offer instead of chasing a new one every week.
